## The seven steps

1. Set up your book: choose paper colour and ink type; print settings are applied automatically.
2. Pick your trim size: margins, gutter, bleed and print specifications are calculated from your page count.
3. Upload your manuscript: drop in your Word document; chapters, front matter and structure are organized in seconds.
4. Auto format: chapters, front and back matter, spacing, margins, page numbers and TOC are formatted; low-resolution images are flagged before print; irregular spacing and line breaks are cleaned.
5. Style your book: choose a template, customise typography, headers, footers and chapter styles; every change applies across the whole book instantly.
6. Add your cover: upload once; spine width is calculated, the cover is validated at 300 DPI, and print-ready covers are prepared for both KDP and IngramSpark.
7. Export and publish: generate the print-ready PDF and validated EPUB from the same manuscript, plus platform-ready cover files, then upload them to your chosen platforms.

## One manuscript, both formats

You upload the manuscript once. PublishPro prepares the digital and print editions in the same workflow and keeps formatting consistent: typography, templates, chapter styles and headers apply to both the PDF/X-1a paperback and the Kindle EPUB. Note that each output is a separate one-time plan (eBook Express and Standard Book).

## Editing after formatting

You can edit inside the online editor (chapter titles, text, styles) without re-uploading, change templates and styling at any time, and also upload a revised manuscript. Settings are saved; unlimited edits and re-downloads are included. If page count grows after paying, the first 8 extra pages are free.

## Experience needed

None. It was built so authors without design or technical skills can format professionally, while designers still get fine control.

## Speed

Formatting itself takes a few minutes after upload, and downloads are instant once you're happy. (The site's comparison: minutes with PublishPro versus 10 to 30 days for manual formatting.)

## Templates

20+ genre-specific interior templates (fiction, non-fiction, poetry, textbooks, memoir, cookbook, short stories, children's books, graphic novels, literary, commercial, academic). One click applies chapter styles, font pairings, dividers and layout settings; everything remains customizable, and you can switch template on an existing project in one click.
