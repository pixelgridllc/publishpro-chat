## eBook workflow: Word to Kindle-ready EPUB

Three steps. Upload: drop in a DOC or DOCX; PublishPro reads the structure, chapters, front matter and back matter automatically, with no pre-formatting required. Format: it auto-generates the table of contents, detects every chapter and structures the book for EPUB, KDP-compliant, in minutes. Export: pick a template, apply fonts and typography styles, then download the validated EPUB, ready for every major eBook platform.

## What the eBook formatter handles

- Smart layout detection of chapters, front matter and back matter, each placed correctly.
- Auto-generated table of contents from chapter headings. Add, rename or delete a chapter and the TOC updates instantly.
- Paragraph formatting: indentation, spacing, alignment and paragraph styles applied consistently across the book.
- EPUB validation before export, optimized for Kindle, Apple Books, Kobo and other reading platforms.
- Clean-up: irregular spacing, unnecessary line breaks and common Word formatting inconsistencies are detected and cleaned.
- Images are embedded and validated during export so they display correctly on supported devices.
- 20+ eBook templates (fiction, non-fiction, children's books and more) for consistent chapter openings, spacing and typography.
- The output is a reflowable EPUB, which adapts to different screen sizes.

## eBook cover

Upload your finished front cover and PublishPro embeds it inside the EPUB so it displays correctly on Kindle, Apple Books, Kobo and other stores. It applies KDP's cover specification (minimum 2,560 × 1,600 pixels, 300 DPI). You still upload the cover image separately on KDP's own form as the store listing cover.

## eBook preview

Before exporting you can preview how the eBook appears on Kindle devices and EPUB readers: chapter layout and navigation, spacing, headings, scene breaks, the table of contents and internal links.
