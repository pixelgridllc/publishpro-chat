## Publishing agencies and teams

PublishPro automates the repetitive production work (margins, gutter, TOC rebuilding, compliance checks) so teams format more books with consistent output. Key points: one account for the whole team with no per-seat charge; per-project settings for trim size, template, typography and export so client standards are respected; pay per book with no forced monthly fee, plus bundle plans with volume discounts (20% to 35%) and 1-year rollover; works for two books a month or twenty. It complements an existing editorial workflow: once editing is done, upload, choose a template, review the preview and export. Integrations with agency software are not published; route those questions to the team. Page: https://publishpro.app/solutions/for-agencies

## Book designers and formatters

Fine typographic control (200+ fonts, drop caps, leading, kerning, chapter title styles, headers and footers per left/right page), 300 DPI image validation, full-bleed positioning, CMYK output for print, custom SVG ornaments for scene breaks, global style changes in one click across a 400-page book, automatic widow and orphan handling, and both formats kept in sync. The site estimates about 12 hours saved per book compared with manual formatting. Page: https://publishpro.app/solutions/for-book-designers

## Self-publishers and first-time authors

No technical skills needed. Upload a Word file, choose a trim size, preview, and download KDP- and IngramSpark-ready files. Common problems it fixes: margins and gutter that don't scale with page count, broken chapter structure, inconsistent fonts and spacing, low-resolution images, spine text too close to the edge, and covers that don't match the interior. Page: https://publishpro.app/solutions/for-self-publishers

## Ghostwriters and book coaches

Deliver fully formatted, publish-ready files to clients without extra editing. Same workflow and pricing as authors; bundles help with several client books.
