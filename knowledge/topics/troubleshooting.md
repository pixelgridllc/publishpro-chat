## Common Kindle eBook rejection causes

A table of contents that doesn't link (chapter titles not using real headings), files exported from Word that Kindle can't parse cleanly, missing or broken chapter navigation, inconsistent typography, low-resolution or oversized images, and compatibility problems across Kindle devices. PublishPro generates a clickable TOC from detected chapters, validates the EPUB and checks images before export.

## Common paperback rejection causes

Margins and gutter that are too small for the page count (they must grow with page count), content outside the safe zone, bleed set incorrectly for edge-to-edge images, low-resolution images (under 300 DPI), fonts not embedded, spine text too close to the edge (KDP requires 0.0625" clearance), and a cover whose dimensions don't match the interior's trim size and page count. PublishPro calculates and checks all of these before export.

## When a customer has a problem with PublishPro itself

Account-specific, billing, export or bug problems can't be diagnosed from the chat. Gather a short description (what they did, what happened, which output and platform, any rejection message), then connect them with the team. The team asks for file details and screenshots. Under the refund policy, formatting defects should be reported to support@publishpro.app within 14 days of the export.

## When a store rejects a book formatted with PublishPro

Ask for the exact message from KDP or IngramSpark. Formatting-related messages should go to the PublishPro team with the rejection email and screenshots. Some rejection reasons are outside formatting (metadata, content, rights, cover text or a missing ISBN), and the platform makes the final call.
