## Paperback workflow

Upload your Word manuscript, select a trim size, and choose paper colour and ink type. PublishPro automatically configures margins, inside gutter and bleed, generates the table of contents, fixes page breaks and structures the book. Then you choose an interior template, adjust fonts and typography, and download the PDF/X-1a print-ready file.

## Print settings

- Ink type: whether images are formatted in colour or greyscale throughout the interior.
- Paper colour: white or cream, which affects how typography and images render.
- Reading direction: sets page flow, gutter position and interior layout structure for your language.

## Trim size

Trim size affects page count, printing cost, readability and the feel of the book. All standard KDP and IngramSpark trim sizes are supported (for example 5×8, 6×9, 7×10, 8.5×8.5), genre-specific recommendations are built in, and you can add a custom trim size. Changing trim size later regenerates layout, margins, gutter, TOC and cover specifications automatically; you don't start over.

## Margins and gutter (automatic)

Margins and gutter are the specs behind most rejected paperbacks, and they are not fixed numbers: the inside gutter must grow with page count because more paper disappears into the spine. PublishPro sets outside margins from the trim size and calculates the gutter from page count, and updates both whenever trim size or page count changes. Example from PublishPro's guide: KDP's minimum inside margin is 0.375" for a 150-page paperback and 0.5" for a 300-page paperback.

## Bleed

Bleed is off by default, which is correct for most text-only paperbacks. Turn bleed on for edge-to-edge images and PublishPro adjusts page size and margins and extends full-page images to the bleed line. With bleed off, images are kept inside the safe zone. Images can be replaced without breaking the layout.

## Interior design controls

200+ fonts with control of size, colour and style for body text, chapter titles and headings; one-click chapter title styles; header and footer templates with separate control for left and right pages; image placement with resolution checks and print-ready conversion; paragraph formatting (indentation, spacing, line height, alignment); ready-made or custom scene breaks and dividers; automatic front and back matter detection (title page, copyright, dedication, prologue, chapters, appendix and more); TOC templates that update when chapters change. ## Professional layout rules handled automatically

New chapters start on the right-hand (recto) page and blank left-hand (verso) pages are inserted when needed. Widows and orphans are detected and page flow adjusted. Irregular spacing and line breaks are cleaned on import. Final TOC page numbers refresh when the PDF is generated.

## Paperback cover setup

Upload your cover artwork and PublishPro calculates spine width from page count and paper type, sizes the full cover to your trim size, checks resolution against 300 DPI and spine-text clearance, and produces a print-ready cover file for KDP and for IngramSpark. Interior and cover are sized together so they don't mismatch. KDP requires 0.0625" clear space around spine text.

## Academic and complex books

For academic manuscripts, heading levels are applied consistently across chapters, sections and subsections, footnotes and reference sections are kept without breaking the layout, and tables, charts and figures are numbered and placed cleanly.
