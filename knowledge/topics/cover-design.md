## Two different cover offerings

1. Cover formatting (inside the formatting app): you upload cover artwork you already have, and PublishPro sizes it. For paperbacks it calculates spine width, bleed and full-wrap dimensions from trim size, page count, paper type and platform. For eBooks it embeds the cover in the EPUB.
2. Custom cover design (a separate paid service): PublishPro's in-house human designers create a cover from scratch.

## Custom cover design process

Step 1, share your vision: tell the team about the book, genre, audience and the style you have in mind. Step 2, design and refine: they create the cover, collect feedback and refine it with you. Step 3, get your cover: the finished cover is prepared for your selected publishing format. To start, create an account and place the order (https://publishpro.app/product/book-cover-design).

## Cover design prices and what's included

One-time payment: eBook cover $199, paperback cover $299 (full wrap with front, spine and back), hardcover cover $399 (case wrap or dust jacket), audiobook cover $49 (square). About three revision rounds are included, with flexibility if a project genuinely needs more. Files delivered: paperback wraparound, a separate eBook cover, final JPEG and PDF files, and print-ready CMYK files with fonts embedded where applicable.

## Cover design promises

Every cover is 100% original and human-made: no AI generators, no resold templates. You get full commercial rights to the finished cover for KDP, IngramSpark, Apple Books or anywhere else. All stock images and fonts used are commercially licensed. Genres highlighted: fantasy, non-fiction, children's, academic and memoir. Support continues after delivery if an upload to KDP runs into a cover issue.

## Cover timing advice

Order the print wraparound once the interior's final page count is known, because spine width depends on page count. Cover research and the brief can start earlier. Turnaround times for cover design are not published; the team can confirm.
