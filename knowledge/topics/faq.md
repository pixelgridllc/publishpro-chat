## How long does formatting take?

A few minutes after uploading the manuscript; files download instantly once you're happy.

## Can I make changes after formatting?

Yes. Edit in the online editor, change templates and styles, or upload a revised manuscript. Unlimited edits and re-downloads are included, and the first 8 extra pages after payment are free.

## Can I preview before paying or downloading?

Yes. Creating and previewing the formatted book is free, no credit card needed. The eBook preview shows how the book looks on Kindle devices and EPUB readers; the paperback preview shows the print layout.

## Will it work for my genre?

Templates cover fiction, non-fiction, poetry, textbooks, memoir, cookbooks, short stories, children's books, graphic novels, literary, commercial and academic. Image-heavy books get 300 DPI checks, full-bleed support and CMYK output. Academic books get consistent heading levels, footnotes and references, and numbered tables and figures. For a picture-book-specific workflow, PublishPro's guide covers KDP setup: https://publishpro.app/blog/how-to-format-a-childrens-book-for-kdp. For very specific layout needs, suggest previewing their own manuscript free, or checking with the team.

## Does it work on my computer?

Yes. It runs in a web browser on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS. A desktop or laptop is the practical choice for formatting a whole book.

## Are my files secure? Do you use my book for AI?

Files are stored securely and remain yours. PublishPro never uses manuscripts to train AI models, and formatting is done by a rule engine, not generative AI.

## Do I own the result?

Yes, you own your book and your files. Custom covers come with full commercial rights.

## How do I publish after formatting?

Download the files and upload them to the platforms yourself (for example KDP and IngramSpark). PublishPro doesn't publish on your behalf.
