## Vellum

A well-regarded formatting app that runs on macOS only, so Windows users can't run it natively. You can format before paying to export. PublishPro's blog cited third-party prices in October 2026 of $199.99 for eBook exports or $249.99 for eBook plus print. Fair framing: if you already have a Mac and like Vellum, it's a strong option. PublishPro suits people who want to work in a browser on any computer, want KDP and IngramSpark validation before upload, prefer paying per book, or want the custom cover service.

## Atticus

Cross-platform writing and formatting software, generally a one-time purchase (reported at $147 in October 2026). Good for people who want to write and format in one place.

## Reedsy Studio

A free, browser-based writing and formatting tool, a low-cost starting point. Check whether its exports and customization meet your print needs.

## Kindle Create

Amazon's free tool, aimed at straightforward Kindle books for KDP. Its scope is narrower for wide distribution or a specific print-ready PDF workflow.

## Manual formatting in Word or InDesign

PublishPro's comparison: manual formatting typically takes 10 to 30 days and needs margins, TOC and front matter set up by hand, plus two separate workflows for eBook and print. PublishPro automates those steps and checks files before upload.

## How to answer "is PublishPro better than X?"

It depends on the author's computer, budget, formats and how much control they want. Give an honest one-line view of the other tool, the specific situations where PublishPro fits, and ask what matters most to them.
