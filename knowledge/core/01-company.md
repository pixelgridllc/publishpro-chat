## What PublishPro is

PublishPro is a browser-based book formatting platform for self-publishing. You upload a Word manuscript and PublishPro turns it into publish-ready files: a Kindle-ready, reflowable EPUB for eBook stores and a print-ready PDF/X-1a interior (plus a print-ready cover file) for paperbacks. Tagline on the site: "Upload your manuscript and download a publish ready book in minutes."

It works in any modern web browser (Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS). There is nothing to install.

PublishPro also sells a separate, human-made custom book cover design service (see the cover design knowledge file).

## Trust promises PublishPro makes publicly

- "Your book is formatted by PublishPro formatting rule engine, not generated by AI." Formatting is rule-based, not generative.
- "We never use your manuscripts to train AI models." Manuscripts are private and secure.
- "You wrote it. You own it. It's 100% yours."
- Custom covers are made by in-house human designers using licensed assets, never AI-generated and never resold templates.

## Who it is for

Website audience pages and copy name: first-time and independent authors, self-publishers, book designers and formatters, ghostwriters, publishing teams and small publishing houses, and publishing agencies. Book coaches are not named on the site, but they can use it for the same reasons as ghostwriters (delivering formatted files to clients).
