## How pricing works

- PublishPro is NOT a subscription for individual books. You pay once per book. No recurring charges, no monthly fees.
- Starting is free: you can create and preview your formatted book for free, no credit card required. You pay only when you are happy with the formatted preview.
- Each pay-per-book plan formats one output type. Publishing both an eBook and a paperback means buying both plans for that book.
- Every plan includes unlimited edits and re-downloads, and 20+ interior templates.

## Per-book plans (one-time payment, USD)

- eBook Express: $9 per book. Produces a Kindle-ready EPUB for Kindle and other eBook platforms. DOC or DOCX input. You can add chapters anytime.
- Standard Book: from $29 to $89 per book; the price depends on page count. Produces a PDF/X-1a print-ready paperback interior for KDP and IngramSpark, plus a print-ready cover file for each platform, sized to the exact trim, spine and bleed. The first 8 extra pages after payment are included free.

## Bundle plans (for publishing teams, agencies and prolific authors)

- Paperback bundle: $24 per paperback before volume discount.
- eBook bundle: $7.50 per eBook before volume discount.
- Bundles can be bought one-time or monthly.
- Volume discounts: 20% on 3 to 10 books, 25% on 11 to 20, 30% on 21 to 30, 35% on 31 to 60 books.
- Unused downloads roll over and stay valid for 1 year.
- Agencies: PublishPro does not charge per seat; one account can be used by the whole team.

## Custom cover design (separate human design service, one-time payment)

- eBook cover: $199 (front cover, optimized for digital display, eBook-ready files)
- Paperback cover: $299 (full wrap: front, spine and back, designed around your trim size, print-ready)
- Hardcover cover: $399 (full cover design, case wrap or dust jacket)
- Audiobook cover: $49 (square cover, digital-ready)
- About three revision rounds included.

## Pricing FAQ (from the site)

- "Is this a subscription?" No. Pay once per book.
- "What if my page count changes after I pay?" The first 8 extra pages are included free on every pay-per-book plan.
- "Can I re-download my files later?" Yes, unlimited edits and re-downloads.
- "Do I need to buy both plans for eBook and paperback?" Yes if publishing both; eBook Express produces the EPUB, Standard Book produces the print-ready PDF/X-1a and cover.

## Rules for talking about price

- Quote these published prices when asked, and point to https://publishpro.app/pricing as the place to confirm current prices before buying, because prices can change.
- The exact Standard Book price for a specific page count is not published in a table here. Say it depends on page count within the $29 to $89 range and that the app shows the exact price for their book.
- Never invent discounts, coupon codes, free trials with day limits, or promotions. The site does not publish a time-limited free trial; it says you can create and preview for free and pay when happy.
- Refunds: see the policies knowledge file (all sales are generally final after export; genuine platform defects are fixed or refunded).
