## What you upload

- Word manuscripts: DOC and DOCX. No pre-formatting or conversion required.
- Google Docs: the site mentions Google Docs-based workflows. The safe, always-correct instruction is: in Google Docs choose File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx), then upload that file.
- Your own cover artwork (for PublishPro to size and prepare for print, or to embed in the eBook).
- Images inside the manuscript (checked against 300 DPI for print).
- Custom ornaments/graphics (including SVGs) for scene breaks and chapter starts.

## What you get

- eBook: a validated, reflowable EPUB, Kindle-ready, with clickable table of contents and navigation, and your cover embedded.
- Paperback: a print-ready PDF/X-1a interior with embedded fonts and CMYK colour output, plus a print-ready cover file sized to the trim, spine and bleed.
- Both formats can come from one manuscript upload; changes stay in sync across both.

PublishPro does NOT export KPF (Kindle Create's format) and does not produce MOBI. Amazon KDP accepts EPUB for Kindle eBooks, so the EPUB is what you upload to KDP. Do not claim KPF export.

## Publishing platforms PublishPro formats for

Named on the site: Amazon KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing), IngramSpark, Apple Books, Kobo (Kobo Writing Life), Google Play Books, Barnes & Noble Press, Draft2Digital, Smashwords, BookBaby and Lulu.

- Print validation is specifically built around Amazon KDP and IngramSpark print requirements (margins, gutter, bleed, trim sizes, fonts, cover dimensions).
- The EPUB is built for Kindle and is compatible with Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play Books and other EPUB stores.
- PublishPro prepares files. It does not publish or upload the book to stores for you, does not act as a distributor, and does not supply ISBNs. You upload the files to each platform yourself.

## Trim sizes

All standard KDP and IngramSpark trim sizes are supported (examples on the site: 4×6, 5×8, 5.25×8, 6×9, 7×10, 8.5×8.5). Genre-specific trim size recommendations are built in. You can also add a custom trim size and PublishPro calculates margins and gutter for it.
